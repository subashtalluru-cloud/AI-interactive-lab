The robotics data gap

Language models learn from trillions of tokens of text. Robots learn actions from demonstrations that someone had to perform. Compare the scales.

What to notice

10,000 hours at 50 steps per second = 1.8 × 10⁹ action steps — about 8,000 times fewer than 15 trillion text tokens (Math Box 176.1).

This is why robot foundation models borrow heavily from vision-language pretraining, internet video and simulation: the knowledge of what objects are and how they behave comes from data that contains no robot actions at all (Chapters 175–176).
