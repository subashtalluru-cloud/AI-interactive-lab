What a matrix does to space

A 2 × 2 matrix sends (1, 0) to its first column and (0, 1) to its second; every other vector follows as a weighted sum. Edit the matrix and watch the whole grid deform.

What to notice

M with columns (1, 1) and (−1, 2) sends (3, 1) to 3·(1, 1) + 1·(−1, 2) = (2, 5) (Math Box 24.1).

The determinant is how much M scales areas: the unit square becomes a parallelogram of that area, negative if flipped. At determinant 0 the plane is flattened onto a line and information is lost — no matrix can undo it. Every layer of a neural network begins with exactly this operation (Chapters 24–25).
