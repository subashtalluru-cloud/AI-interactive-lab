What images cost a multimodal model

An image enters a language model as a run of visual tokens. The projector that bridges vision and language is tiny; the tokens are not. Count what a question with images costs in context, attention and memory.

What to notice

336 × 336 with 14-pixel patches: 576 visual tokens; a 30-token question then costs 606, 95% of it image. The linear projector has 4.2 M parameters, the MLP 21 M — tiny beside a 7B language model. A 64-query resampler cuts 576 to 64 (Math Box 120.1).

Thirty frames of video at this resolution need about 17,000 tokens; attention cost grows with the square. Compressing visual tokens saves compute but can lose fine detail — for a small retinal lesion, sometimes the detail that mattered (Chapters 118, 120, 123).
