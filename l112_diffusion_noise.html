The forward process of a diffusion model

A drawn fundus, destroyed one noise level at a time by the formula x_t = √ᾱ · x₀ + √(1 − ᾱ) · ε. A diffusion model is trained to look at any of these noisy images and say which part is the noise.

What to notice

At ᾱ = 0.5 the bright disc and the dark macula are still visible under the static, while the thin vessels are already hard to see: large structures survive heavy noise, fine detail does not. That is why, when generating, early denoising steps decide the overall layout and late steps add fine detail.

The weights √ᾱ and √(1 − ᾱ) keep the total variance constant, so the noisy image never blows up or fades out — only the balance between signal and noise moves. This is the same move as the reparameterization trick of the VAE (Chapter 110): a sample written as a scaled signal plus scaled noise.
