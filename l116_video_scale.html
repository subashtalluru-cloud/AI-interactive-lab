The scale of video

Five seconds of video is tens of millions of numbers. Video generators work in a compressed latent space and cut it into spacetime patches — and even then, full attention between every pair of patches is enormous.

What to notice

5 s at 24 fps, 512 × 512: 94 million numbers; latent at 8× spatial, 4× temporal, 16 channels: 2.0 million (≈ 48×); 2 × 2 patches give 30,720 spacetime tokens and 9.4 × 10⁸ attention scores per head per layer (Math Box 116.1).

Double the duration and the attention cost quadruples. This is why long, consistent video remains hard, and why many systems use factorized or windowed attention rather than full spacetime attention (Chapters 66, 113, 116).
