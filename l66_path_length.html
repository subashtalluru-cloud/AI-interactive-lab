Sequential steps and path lengths

How far apart can two words be before a model struggles to connect them? Compare how many sequential steps each architecture needs, and how many hops separate the first token from the last.

What to notice

1,000 tokens: an RNN needs 1,000 sequential steps and a 999-step path; a width-5 convolution stack needs about 250 layers before the ends meet; self-attention connects them in one step (Math Box 66.1).

One hop means gradients and information do not have to survive a long chain — no vanishing along the way (Chapter 54). The price is that every position compares with every other: n² scores per head per layer, which is why long contexts are expensive (Chapters 66, 167).
