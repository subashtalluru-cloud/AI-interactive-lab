Four activation functions, and their slopes

An activation shapes both what a neuron outputs and how much gradient flows back through it. Move the pre-activation z and compare.

What to notice

At z = 3: sigmoid 0.953 with slope 0.045; tanh 0.995 with slope 0.010; ReLU 3 with slope 1 (Math Box 36.1). Saturated sigmoid and tanh units pass almost no gradient.

The last line multiplies one slope per layer. The best a sigmoid can ever do is 0.25 per layer — 0.25¹⁰ ≈ 10⁻⁶ — which is why deep sigmoid networks were so hard to train and ReLU (slope exactly 1 when active) helped deep learning take off. Leaky ReLU keeps a small slope for negative z so units cannot die completely (Chapters 36, 54).
