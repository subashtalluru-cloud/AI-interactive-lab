A small tilt with large effects

Post-training often changes a model by only a little per token — but at the first few tokens, a small logit shift can change which kind of response begins, and everything after follows from that start.

What to notice

From 0.15, a tilt of 2.0 gives 0.15 × e² / (0.15 × e² + 0.85) ≈ 0.57 (Math Box 97.1). A modest change to one logit turns a minority behaviour into the majority.

Because generation is autoregressive, once the answer has begun directly, the base model’s own knowledge makes a direct continuation likely. This is one reason post-training can reshape behaviour so much while changing the model’s knowledge so little — and why its effects can be shallow and easy to undo (Chapters 89, 97, 157).
