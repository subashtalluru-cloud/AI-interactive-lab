What fits on a clinic workstation?

Local models keep patient data on site. Whether one fits depends on the size of the weights, and how fast it runs depends on how quickly those weights can be read from memory for every token.

What to notice

3B at 4 bits ≈ 1.5 GB; at 100 GB/s about 15 ms per token, ~65 tokens/s. 70B at 4 bits ≈ 35 GB and ~3 tokens/s. Training the 3B model on 10T tokens costs about 1.8 × 10²³ FLOPs (Math Box 169.1).

Small models move cost from inference to training: they are over-trained far beyond the compute-optimal ratio so that every later use is cheap. For many clinical extraction and summarization tasks, a well-evaluated small local model can be the safer and cheaper choice (Chapters 84, 88, 100, 169).
