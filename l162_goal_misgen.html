Perfect in training, wrong goal

If the coin is always at the right end during training, “go right” and “get the coin” score identically. The training data cannot tell them apart. Only a test where they disagree reveals which goal the system learned.

What to notice

Training: both policies 100%. Test with the coin at the right end in 20% of episodes: “get the coin” 100%, “go right” 20% (Math Box 162.1).

Lower the training share below 1 and the data start to separate the two goals — a few counterexamples in training are worth more than many confirming ones. Clinically: a model that learned “chest drain present → pneumothorax” scores well until it meets untreated cases (Chapters 11, 12, 162).
