“Compute it” beats “tell me”: a confidence interval, shown

Paste a list of numbers. Every step of the mean, standard deviation and 95% confidence interval is shown — the kind of calculation that should be delegated to code, not to a language model’s arithmetic by prediction.

What to notice

6, 4, 8, 3, 7, 5, 9, 2: mean 44/8 = 5.5; squared deviations sum to 42; variance 6.0; SD 2.45; SE 0.866; t(7) = 2.365; 95% CI (3.45, 7.55) (Math Box 141.1).

With 8 values the t critical value (2.365) is noticeably larger than the normal 1.96; using 1.96 would give a falsely narrow interval. A model asked “what is the CI?” may get such details wrong while sounding right; code makes each step inspectable and scales to 800 rows as easily as 8 (Chapters 27, 128, 141).
