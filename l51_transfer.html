Transfer learning: how much must be learned?

A network pretrained on millions of images already knows edges, textures and shapes. Fine-tuning for a new clinical task may need to fit only a small head — or a few layers — rather than everything from scratch.

What to notice

ResNet-18, 512 features → 2 classes: 1,026 trainable parameters, about two images per parameter with 2,000 images. From scratch: 11.2 million parameters, over 5,000 per image (Math Box 51.1).

Fine-tuning a whole stage has many parameters but they start from sensible values, and a small learning rate with early stopping keeps them near. With large target datasets the advantage of pretraining shrinks, and training from scratch can catch up [WELL SUPPORTED] (Chapters 20, 43, 51).
