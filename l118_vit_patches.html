Turning an image into tokens

A vision transformer cuts the image into square patches, flattens each one and projects it to a vector. The patch size sets the sequence length, and the sequence length sets the cost.

What to notice

224 × 224 with 16-pixel patches: 14 × 14 = 196 patches, 197 tokens with [CLS]; the patch-embedding layer has 16 × 16 × 3 × 768 = 589,824 weights. 336 × 336 with 14-pixel patches: 576 tokens.

Halving the patch size or doubling the resolution quadruples the tokens and multiplies attention cost by about sixteen. For medical images, where a microaneurysm or a disc haemorrhage may be a few pixels, this is the central trade-off: fine detail against compute (Chapters 48, 118, 123).
