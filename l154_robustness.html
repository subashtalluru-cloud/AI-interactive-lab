What to notice

92% clean; 88% blur; 90% JPEG; 79% small-pupil vignetting — a 14% relative drop. Correct on all five phrasings for only 130/200 = 65% of questions, though a single fixed phrasing might report 84% (Math Box 154.1).

Report the worst relevant corruption, not only the average, and consistency across phrasings, not only accuracy under one. Which corruption matters depends on the clinic: vignetting dominates where small pupils are common (Chapters 153–155).
