What to notice

Sliding “add the same number to every logit” changes nothing at all. Only the differences between logits matter, which is why real implementations subtract the largest logit before exponentiating — it keeps the arithmetic inside the range a computer can represent, without changing the answer.

Low temperature concentrates probability on the leading token: confident, repeatable, conventional text. High temperature flattens the distribution and gives unlikely tokens a real chance — more variety, and more errors. At T→0 the distribution becomes a hard maximum; at large T it approaches uniform.

Entropy, shown above in nats, measures how uncertain the distribution is. The maximum for five options is ln 5 ≈ 1.61.
