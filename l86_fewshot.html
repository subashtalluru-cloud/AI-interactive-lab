Few-shot prompting: shifting the distribution without training

Examples in the prompt change nothing in the model’s weights. They change the context — and so the next-token distribution. Add examples one at a time and watch the intended continuation take over.

Probabilities are illustrative, following Math Box 86.1; each example is modelled as adding a fixed boost to the log-odds of the pattern it demonstrates.

What to notice

Zero-shot: “ intraocular” 0.35. With two examples of the Abbreviation → Expansion pattern: about 0.93 (Math Box 86.1).

The examples also teach format, length and label balance — including biases you did not intend (Chapter 137). This is in-context learning: a change of input, not of parameters (Chapters 86, 137).
