What a months-long lag means

Open-weight models trail the frontier by some months. How much capability is that? Under a simple assumption — the frontier closes half its remaining gap to a ceiling every fixed period — a time lag converts into points on a benchmark.

The growth curve is an illustrative assumption from Chapter 172, not a measured law. [SPECULATIVE]

What to notice

From 80% with a 12-month half-gap: 90% in a year, 84.1% in 4 months; a model 4 months behind scores about 74.8% (Math Box 172.1).

Near a benchmark’s ceiling the same lag costs fewer points, so time gaps become hard to measure exactly when they are most discussed. Estimates of the lag in 2025–26 range from about four months to under a year depending on method and domain (Chapter 172).
