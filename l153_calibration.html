When the model says 95%, is it right 95% of the time?

Two thousand simulated predictions from an overconfident classifier — the typical behaviour of modern deep networks. The reliability diagram shows the gap; one number, the temperature, closes most of it.

What to notice

Temperature scaling divides every logit by one number. It cannot change which cases are ranked higher — the AUC stays exactly the same — yet it can remove most of the calibration error. Ranking (discrimination) and honest probabilities (calibration) are different properties.

Set the overconfidence to 1: the model is calibrated at T = 1, and any other temperature makes it worse. Calibration found on one population can be lost on another (Chapter 158) — the best temperature must be chosen on data like the deployment data.
