What to notice

With the book's values the forward pass gives p = 0.5764 and loss 0.551. One step at η = 0.5 raises p to 0.668 and lowers the loss to 0.404 — every weight moved in the direction its gradient indicated, by an amount proportional to its share of the blame.

Each hidden gradient is the output's error (p − y) times the connecting weight times σ′ = h(1 − h). That product is the chain rule of Chapter 31, and it is why sigmoid networks learn slowly when units saturate: σ′ is never more than 0.25.

Press “Update weights” repeatedly. The loss keeps falling but by less each time, and with η = 3 it can overshoot — the same learning-rate behaviour as the one-dimensional bowl in Lab L15.2.
