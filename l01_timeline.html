A timeline of the field

Eighty years of artificial intelligence, as alternating waves of rules and learning, optimism and winter. Filter by kind of idea and follow each event to the chapter that explains it.

    What to notice

    The two traditions ran side by side from the start: the perceptron (learning) is almost as old as the Dartmouth proposal (rules). Each winter followed promises that outran what the methods of the day could deliver.

    The learning tradition won not because rules were wrong, but because data and compute eventually made learning cheaper than writing knowledge by hand — the “flip” of Chapter 12. Whether today’s pace continues is an open question (Chapter 180).
