Self-supervision: labels for free

Unlabelled text contains its own targets: every word is the answer to “what comes next?” given the words before it. One sentence of T tokens yields T − 1 training examples, and a trillion-token corpus yields about a trillion.

What to notice

“raised intraocular pressure causes damage” (T = 5) gives 4 pairs: raised → intraocular; raised intraocular → pressure; … → damage (Math Box 14.1).

No clinician labelled anything. This is why language models could scale where supervised medical datasets could not: the labels were already in the data. The same trick powers masked-image and contrastive pretraining (Chapters 14, 77, 119).
