Prompting, fine-tuning or retrieval: a cost comparison at scale

At a handful of requests, the cost of a long prompt is irrelevant. At a hundred thousand a month it can dominate — and then the one-off cost of fine-tuning a smaller model may pay for itself. Prices are illustrative; the structure of the comparison is the point.

What to notice

100,000 letters a month: the large model with a 5,000-token prompt costs about $1,950/month; a small fine-tuned model at one-tenth the price with a 1,500-token prompt about $90 (Math Box 146.1).

The saving only counts after the one-off costs of curating data, training and validating — and the fine-tuned model must be re-validated whenever the task or guidance changes. Retrieval costs resemble the long-prompt option per request, but keeps content current and cited. The right choice depends on volume, stability and the need for provenance (Chapters 98, 146).
