Distillation: softening the teacher’s answer

A large teacher’s probabilities contain more than its top answer: how similar the wrong classes are. Raising the temperature T makes that similarity visible, so the student can learn from it.

What to notice

Logits (4, 2, 0.5): at T = 1 the teacher gives (0.858, 0.116, 0.026); at T = 4, (0.494, 0.300, 0.206). The ranking is unchanged, but “mild is more like moderate than severe is” now carries real weight (Math Box 101.1).

Very high T flattens everything toward uniform and the information fades again; practical values are about 2–10. The T² factor keeps the gradient size comparable across temperatures. The student is used at T = 1 afterwards (Chapter 101).
