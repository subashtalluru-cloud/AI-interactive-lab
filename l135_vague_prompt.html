Counting the choices a vague prompt leaves open

“Write about glaucoma for patients” leaves many decisions unstated. Each one the model resolves toward what is typical in its training. Tick the choices you specify and watch the space of acceptable-looking answers shrink.

What to notice

3 × 3 × 3 × 4 × 3 × 3 × 2 = 1,944 broadly different answers, all “about glaucoma for patients” (Math Box 135.1).

Specify five of the seven and the space collapses to a handful. The model is not being lazy when it produces a middle-length, general, mildly reassuring leaflet — it is filling your unstated choices with the most probable defaults. Specification is what makes the answer yours (Chapters 135–136).
