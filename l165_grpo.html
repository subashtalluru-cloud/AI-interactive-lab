GRPO: learning from a group of attempts

Sample several answers to the same problem, check each, and score each answer against the group’s average. Answers better than their siblings are reinforced; worse ones are discouraged. No separate value network is needed.

What to notice

Rewards (1, 0, 0, 1): mean 0.5, SD 0.5, advantages (+1, −1, −1, +1) (Math Box 165.1).

Push p near 0 or 1: most groups are all-wrong or all-right, advantages vanish, and the problem is wasted compute — so training sets are filtered to problems of intermediate difficulty for the current model. And every token of an answer shares one advantage: a correct answer reached by a lucky wrong step is rewarded whole (Chapter 165).
