What an agent’s trace costs

An agent calls the model once per step, and each call rereads everything so far: instructions, tool descriptions, and every earlier proposal and observation. Cost grows with the square of the number of steps.

What to notice

2,000 initial tokens, 800 added per step, 10 calls: 10 × 2,000 + 800 × (0 + 1 + … + 9) = 20,000 + 36,000 = 56,000 tokens read (Math Box 130.1).

Double the steps and the cost roughly quadruples. Long agent runs are managed by summarizing or pruning the trace, caching the fixed prefix, and delegating sub-tasks to fresh contexts — each with the usual risk of dropping something that mattered (Chapters 129–131). Prices are illustrative.
