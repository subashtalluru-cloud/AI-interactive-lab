One score, many thresholds

What to notice

At a threshold of 0.5 you recover Chapter 21's matrix: TP 3, FP 1, FN 2, TN 4 — sensitivity 0.6, specificity 0.8, precision 0.75.

The ROC curve does not depend on the threshold at all: it is the staircase you trace by sweeping it. Its area, 0.88, is the fraction of the 25 glaucoma–healthy pairs in which the glaucomatous eye gets the higher score — 22 of 25.

No threshold gives zero errors. The IOP values overlap (the eye at 21 mmHg is healthy; the one at 19 is not), which is Chapter 11's lesson: the best any single cut-off can do on these data is two errors.
