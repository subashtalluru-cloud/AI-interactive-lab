The journey of one token

“The IOP is …” — what happens, number by number, between the prompt and the word “high”. A miniature transformer with two dimensions and one block, using the exact values of Chapters 68–75, 73 and 89.

What to notice

Nothing mysterious happens at any step: lookups, dot products, a softmax-weighted average, additions, a small ReLU network, more dot products, one more softmax. “High” wins because the final vector points most nearly along the template for “high”.

Change the embedding of “ IOP”. The attention weights, the residual stream, the logits and the winner all shift: a different context vector produces a different continuation. In a real model, training has placed thousands of such vectors so that the same arithmetic yields sensible text.
