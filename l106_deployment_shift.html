The same model, a different clinic

A referral classifier validated on an enriched test set is deployed in community screening, and later a new camera arrives. The model has not changed; what a positive result means has.

What to notice

Development: PPV 0.70. Screening at 5% prevalence: 0.33 — two in three referrals are false alarms. With the new camera: 0.22, and one true case in five is missed (Math Box 106.1).

Nothing in the model’s code changed between these three panels. That is why the development pipeline (Chapter 106) insists on defining the intended population, evaluating externally, stress-testing under shift and monitoring after deployment: each catches a different step of this slide.
