Deduplication, filtering and the data you actually have

A web crawl sounds enormous until duplicates and low-quality text are removed. What remains sets how many times a model must see the same data — or how much lower-quality or synthetic data it must accept.

What to notice

20T raw → 12T after removing 40% duplicates → 6T after dropping half as low quality. A 15T run then repeats the 6T set about 2.5 times (Math Box 171.1).

A few epochs over good data cost little; many cost more. Aggressive filtering raises average quality but can silently remove dialects, informal writing and whole languages that a “polished-text” classifier rates poorly — a bias built in before training starts (Chapters 158, 170, 171).
