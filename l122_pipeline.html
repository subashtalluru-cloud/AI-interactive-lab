Pipeline or native: why a chain of models loses time and information

A voice assistant can be three models in a row — speech-to-text, a language model, text-to-speech — or one model that hears and speaks directly. Add up the delays, and notice what the middle model never hears.

What to notice

0.5 + 0.5 + 0.3 = 1.3 s for the pipeline; about 0.32 s reported for a native speech model (Math Box 122.1; the native figure is vendor-reported).

The pipeline’s language model sees only a transcript: no tone, emphasis, hesitation or second speaker. Native models keep that information — at the cost of thousands of audio tokens per minute (Chapters 121, 122).
