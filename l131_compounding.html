How errors compound over many steps

An agent that is right 95% of the time on each action sounds reliable. Over twenty actions, it usually is not. Set the per-step reliability, the number of steps, and how many errors your checks catch.

What to notice

At 95% per step and 20 steps, only about 36% of runs succeed end to end. Catching half the errors raises this to about 60% — more than a model upgrade from 95% to 97% per step would.

This is why reliable agents depend on checks anchored to something outside the model — tests, tool results, sources, people — rather than on a model that never errs (Chapter 131). The same multiplication explains vanishing gradients (Chapter 54) and drift in imagined plans (Chapter 175).
