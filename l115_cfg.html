Classifier-free guidance: extrapolating towards the prompt

At every denoising step an image model makes two predictions: one ignoring the prompt, one using it. Guidance takes the difference — “what the prompt changes” — and scales it up. Beyond a scale of 1 the model extrapolates past its own conditional prediction.

What to notice

(0.2, −0.1) and (0.5, 0.3): w = 1 gives (0.5, 0.3) — exactly the conditional; w = 3 gives (1.1, 1.1); w = 7.5 gives (2.45, 2.9) (Math Box 115.1).

Higher guidance makes images match the prompt more strongly and look more “typical”, at the cost of diversity and, pushed too far, of oversaturated or distorted images — much like lowering the temperature of a language model (Chapters 70, 112, 115).
