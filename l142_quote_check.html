Why asking for quotations makes checking cheap

The form in which you ask for evidence decides how expensive it is to verify. Checking that cited papers exist and support the claims is research; checking that a quoted sentence appears in a supplied document is a string search.

What to notice

Twenty claims: full citation checking takes about 100–200 minutes; quotation checking against supplied documents about 10 (Math Box 142.1).

A hasty check that references merely exist catches only fabricated sources and misses misattributions — often the larger share. Quotation checking catches both: a fabricated quote fails the search; a misattributed claim is visibly unsupported by its quote. Times are illustrative; the ratio is the point (Chapters 138, 142, 152).
