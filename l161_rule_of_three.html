What finding nothing proves

A red team makes n attempts and finds no failures. That does not show the failure rate is zero — only that it is probably below about 3/n. And a failure too rare to show up in testing can still happen many times a month in deployment.

What to notice

500 clean attempts bound the failure rate at 3/500 = 0.6%. To have a 95% chance of seeing a 1-in-1,000 failure takes about 3,000 attempts. A 0.1% serious-failure rate over a million uses a month is about 1,000 failures a month (Math Box 161.1).

Red teaming is good at finding failures and poor at proving their absence. Report it as “in a search of this size and kind we found these problems and no others” — never “the system is safe”. Skilled attackers are not random samples: they find failures faster than chance would, so red-team rates are not the rates ordinary users will meet.
