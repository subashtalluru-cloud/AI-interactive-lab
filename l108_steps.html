The cost of generating one piece at a time

An autoregressive generator makes one piece per step, and each step must wait for the one before. For text, pieces are words. For raw images and audio there are far more pieces — unless the data are first compressed into tokens.

What to notice

A 256 × 256 colour image pixel by pixel: 196,608 steps. As a 32 × 32 grid of codes: 1,024 steps — about 190× fewer. One second of 16 kHz audio: 16,000 steps; a paragraph of text: about 100 (Math Box 108.1).

This is why modern image, video and audio generators work on compressed tokens or latents (VQ-VAE codes, latent diffusion) rather than raw samples — the same move as tokenizing text into subwords rather than characters (Chapters 79, 108, 113).
