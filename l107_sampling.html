Generating new data: learn a distribution, then sample

The simplest generative model: fit a normal distribution to IOP readings, then produce new readings by transforming random noise. Every image generator follows the same pattern — noise in, learned transformation, sample out — with a far richer transformation.

What to notice

z = 0.8 → 15.5 + 2.5 × 0.8 = 17.5 mmHg; z = −1.2 → 12.5 mmHg (Math Box 107.1). Each belongs to no real patient.

A generator can only produce what its distribution allows: this one never makes an IOP of 45, however many samples it draws, and it cannot know about a subgroup it never saw. The same limits, less visibly, apply to synthetic images and text (Chapters 107, 170).
