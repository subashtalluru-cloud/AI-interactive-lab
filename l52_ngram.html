Why fixed windows fail on language

An n-gram model predicts the next word from the previous n − 1. Make the window longer and the number of possible contexts explodes past any corpus; keep it short and long-range dependencies disappear.

What to notice

V = 50,000: trigram contexts 50,000² = 2.5 × 10⁹; 5-gram contexts 50,000⁴ ≈ 6 × 10¹⁸. With n = 5 the model predicting “stable” never sees “lens” (Math Box 52.1).

Even a trillion-token corpus covers a vanishing fraction of 5-gram contexts, so most are never seen once. Recurrent networks, then attention, escaped the dilemma by compressing or selecting context rather than enumerating it (Chapters 52–53, 66).
