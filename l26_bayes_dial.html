The Bayes dial

A glaucoma screening test with fixed sensitivity and specificity, used in populations with different prevalence. One thousand people, drawn as dots. Watch what happens to the meaning of a positive result.

Presets:

The same arithmetic, three ways

What to notice

At 2% prevalence with sensitivity 0.6 and specificity 0.8 — the book's example — only about 6 in every 100 positives actually have glaucoma (PPV ≈ 0.058). Nothing about the test changed between presets; only who was being tested.

Specificity dominates at low prevalence. Raise specificity to 0.99 at 2% prevalence and PPV jumps far more than raising sensitivity to 0.99 would achieve, because false positives come from the huge healthy group.
